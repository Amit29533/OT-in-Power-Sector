Interactive OT Cyber Incident Decision Lab

Audit Guidelines & Cybersecurity Maturity Roadmap for the power & energy sector.

A progressive operational-technology (OT) cyber-incident simulation where you step into the role of an OT security lead. Work through See → Understand → Decide → Act → Recover as an incident unfolds across the engineering environment, the control network, the SOC, and the IT/OT boundary — then turn the lessons into a measurable maturity roadmap.

01 · Simulate

Progressive Incident

  • Suspicious engineering workstation activity
  • Unauthorized PLC changes
  • AI-generated security alerts
  • IT-to-OT ransomware propagation
02 · Decide

Decision & Action Framework

Practice identifying decision points, assessing operational impact, and choosing containment, escalation, and recovery actions — exactly as you would in a live event.

03 · Improve

Maturity & Roadmap

Assess your program against eight OT security domains, expose critical gaps, and build a quick-win + strategic roadmap aligned to audit expectations.
